Feathered Glass - Light/Dark Variants

Responsive Approach:
Since the overlay appears on top of other apps, we can't detect the background luminance. Instead, we offer:
• Light variant - Best for dark/medium backgrounds
• Dark variant - Works on all backgrounds (recommended for routing)
• Adaptive variants - Designed to work well on any background

💡 Tip: For routing mode, the adaptive dark variants provide the best readability across all app backgrounds.
