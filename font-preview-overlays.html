Creative Overlay Solutions - Maximum Readability

Apple's HIG recommendations for overlays:
• Use backdrop blur with scroll edge effect to keep text readable
• Feathered glass background enhances text readability by adding contrast and blurring details
• System notifications use white with 18% opacity + blur for translucent appearance
• Ensure text has sufficient contrast without implying background content is interactive
